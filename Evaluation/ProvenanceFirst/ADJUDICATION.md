## Decisions, not performance optimization

- Historical fact retrieval may be satisfied by a current revision explicitly
  recording the past fact. Include it in relevant evidence; only genuinely
  original-document questions require a particular prior revision. A flat evidence
  set measures relevant recovery, not alternative minimal proof bundles.
- An authorization question may be answerable negatively even when a voice memo's
  referent is unknown. Development's museum case now asks about the unknown referent.
- An expedition return/completion record is part of expedition history as well as
  inventory management. Its ideal membership and later revision now preserve both.
  Conversely the lending project's root is its general service mandate, not a
  particular expedition reservation mislabeled as lending-only.
- Alternative ordering respects causal derivation; it is not a late-arrival test.
  Authors added missing predecessor decisions and completion dependencies without
  serializing all independent captures. All 24 alternative schedules remain nontrivial.
- The evaluation radio scenario resembled a previously exposed C5/C6 scenario.
  Its entire family was replaced with packaging localization versus physical barcode
  certification, including edition-specific applicability, before the freeze.
- Authors removed selected classifier-verdict phrasing and identified fictional
  capture annotations explicitly. Independent reviewers reassessed changed evidence.
